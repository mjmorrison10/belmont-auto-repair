Our Services

Complete Auto Repair
in Belmont Shore

From routine maintenance to complex engine work, Belmont Auto Repair handles it all. We work on all makes and models: domestic and import. If it has wheels, we can fix it. And if another shop said it can't be done, bring it to us.

Oil Change & Maintenance

Keep your engine healthy with regular oil changes and fluid checks. We use quality oils and filters, and we'll never push services your car doesn't actually need. Honest recommendations, every time.

Brake Service & Repair

Your safety is our priority. We inspect and repair brake pads, rotors, calipers, and brake lines. We'll show you exactly what needs fixing and explain your options clearly. No surprises.

Engine Repair & Diagnostics

Check engine light? Strange noises? Our experienced mechanics diagnose and repair engine problems of all kinds. We never give up on a repair. That's the Belmont difference. Other shops give up. We get it done.

Transmission Repair

Hard shifting, slipping, or leaking? We service both automatic and manual transmissions. We'll give you an honest assessment before recommending any major work. No unnecessary repairs, ever.

Smog Check & Repair

We perform certified smog checks and smog-related repairs. If your vehicle fails, we'll diagnose the emissions issue and get it passing: fast and affordable.

Electrical & A/C

From battery and alternator issues to air conditioning repair, we handle all electrical and climate control problems. Stay cool and confident on the road, year-round.

Tire Sales & Service

New tires, rotations, balancing, and flat repairs. We offer competitive pricing on all major brands and can get you back on the road quickly and safely.

Ignition Interlock (IID)

We provide professional ignition interlock device installation and service through Intoxalock. We handle the process with complete privacy and respect. No judgment. Just quality service.
