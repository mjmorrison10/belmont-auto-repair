What We Do

Our Services

✦

From routine maintenance to the most complex diagnostics, we handle it all with the same level of care and attention. Every service is performed by experienced hands — not trainees, not robots — real mechanics who take pride in their craft.

Oil Change & Maintenance

Regular maintenance done right. We don't just swap fluids — we inspect, advise, and keep your car running at its best for the long haul.

Brake Service & Repair

Your safety depends on your brakes. We use quality parts and take the time to do it properly — no shortcuts, no surprises.

Engine Repair & Diagnostics

This is where we shine. Complex engine problems that stump other shops? That's our specialty. Tony will find what others miss.

Transmission Repair

Transmission trouble is scary. We diagnose it honestly, explain it clearly, and fix it right — whether it needs a rebuild or a simple adjustment.

Smog Check & Repair

California smog requirements are strict. We're certified to test and repair, so if your car doesn't pass, we'll get it there.

Electrical & A/C

Modern cars are computers on wheels. We have the diagnostic tools and the experience to solve electrical and climate control issues.

Tire Sales & Service

Quality tires at fair prices, mounted and balanced by hand. We'll help you find the right rubber for your driving and your budget.

Ignition Interlock (IID)

Via Intoxalock — Professional & Private

We understand this is a personal matter. Our IID installation through Intoxalock is handled with complete discretion and respect. No judgment — just professional, confidential service in a private setting.
